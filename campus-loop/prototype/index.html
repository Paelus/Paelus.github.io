For Studierendenwerke

Reusable cups that work: on the student card, under your brand.

CampusLoop is a white-label cup deposit system for Studierendenwerke. The deposit is booked on the student card, cups can be returned at any participating campus, and you pay per cup instead of a monthly system fee.

Single-use cups are getting more expensive in university towns with a packaging tax, and the PPWR requires a reusable option from about February 2028. CampusLoop helps you run a system that is actually used, not one that only exists on paper.

Built for how campuses actually run

A system, not a cup brand: we provide the cup pool, the campus-card deposit link, return logistics and cross-campus clearing.

1

No app, no cash, just the student card.

Students pay the deposit (proposed: €1, in line with the usual campus deposit) with the card they already carry. There is no registration and no app download, which is a hurdle for spontaneous coffee buyers. Campus-card deposits have already worked in practice, for example at StuCup in Munich.

Integration with your card system is checked together with you before any pilot starts.

2

Pay per cup, not per month.

No monthly minimum and no fixed system fee: you pay a low fee per cup used. Quiet cafeterias do not pay for capacity they do not need.

The exact per-cup price is set during the pilot, based on real washing and logistics costs.

3

One return network across campuses.

The goal: a cup borrowed on Campus A can be returned on Campus B, once both take part. CampusLoop would link participating Studierendenwerke through a shared clearing service, so you are not running an island solution. Already running your own system? We connect it rather than replace it.

4

Your brand stays on the cup.

CampusLoop is white label: cups, return points and communication carry the Studierendenwerk's name. If you prefer to keep your own cups, you can use our software on its own.

Built-in nudges and the data you need.

We provide cashier settings for a reusable discount or a single-use surcharge. Discounts were the most effective nudge in the HWR Berlin survey, and they are legally allowed. You also get usage data to support your VerpackG reporting and to track progress towards the 2030 reuse target.

Start a pilot with 2-4 cafeterias.

In the pilot we would measure what we found no public data on for campuses: return rate, cycles per cup and cost per cycle. You get the results for your board and for your next tender.
