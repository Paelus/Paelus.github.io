    Build your own copilot

    about 10 minutes

    Everything below is one click to copy. You get your own agent on your own WhatsApp; nothing is shared with mine.

    1. Install Cue and sign up with your phone number. Invite code: · Windows: Microsoft Store, Mac / iOS / Android: search “Cue” in your app store.
    2. Create your agent (any name), then open its profile → Messenger → WhatsApp → Connect. Scan the QR code with your phone camera and send the pre-filled message. In the same menu (⋯) turn on Receive all replies.
    3. Add the skill: Settings → Skills → Add → Import from GitHub, paste this link:
      https://github.com/Paelus/Paelus.github.io/tree/main/student-copilot/pforzheim-student-copilot
      No GitHub import? Open SKILL.md, and send it to your agent with “Please save this as a skill”.
    4. Schedule the morning brief: send this to your agent in Cue:
      Create a weekday automation at 07:00 Europe/Berlin named "Student Copilot – morning brief" that runs my pforzheim-student-copilot skill and sends the brief to me via WhatsApp. Then run "brief" once now as a test.
    5. Test it on WhatsApp (in the chat with your agent):
    6. Keep it alive: reply to the brief once a day (a 👍 is enough). WhatsApp only lets a service write within 24 h of your last message.
