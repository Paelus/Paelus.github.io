FAQ

Good to know

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. The session is made for everyday office work. If you can write an email, you can follow along.

Can I join online?

Yes. The session is streamed live at the same time. Choose "Online" when you register and the join link comes with your calendar invitation.

Is lunch provided?

Bring your own lunch. Coffee and water are provided in the 8F Multipurpose Classroom.

Can I use company data with AI tools?

Only with the tools approved by IT. We cover the basic do's and don'ts in the first ten minutes.

Will there be a recording or slides?

Yes, all registered colleagues receive the slides and the prompt examples after the workshop.
