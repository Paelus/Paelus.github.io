# AURUM smart ring – run summary

**Opportunity:** Smart rings grow while wearables overall slow down; the leader (Oura, ~3/4 of shipments) charges a monthly membership and most rings look like gadgets. A design-led, subscription-free ring from Pforzheim's jewellery tradition fills a clear gap.

**Target customer:** design-conscious professionals (28–45) and gift buyers in DE (lead), US (scale), JP (test via Rakuten).

**Positioning:** *Jewellery first. Tech inside.* – pay once, no subscription, your data stays in the EU, free sizing.

**Video:** 120 s, 12 scenes, 16:9, presenter "Mara" – from a Pforzheim goldsmith's bench through a day (sleep, office, run) to the gift moment. Storyboard: `AURUM_Video_Storyboard.xlsx`.

**Open checks (human):** battery/sensor tests, EU hosting claim, returns policy per market, Japanese copy, presenter consent.
