# 01 · Market Research – AURUM smart ring

**Input:** AURUM, a concept smart ring "designed in Pforzheim, Germany's Goldstadt": jewellery-grade titanium with a gold-tone option, sleep / stress / activity tracking, **no subscription**. Markets: Germany (DE), United States (US), Japan (JP). Channel: own D2C shop + Amazon (DE/US) + Rakuten (JP).

## 2 Demand signals (review & social themes)
- **Battery life** is the top complaint (3–5 days, degrading) [source: Best Buy / Digital Trends reviews]
- **Subscription fatigue**: Oura's ~$5.99/month membership is the most-cited annoyance; rivals advertise "no subscription" [source: Bloomberg; Digital Trends]
- **Sizing** problems and inconsistent sizing kits [source: Best Buy reviews; Tom's Guide]
- **Looks**: many rings read as "gadget", not jewellery – opening for a design-led brand [assumption]
- **Privacy** concerns around health data [source: Oura data-policy coverage]

## 4 Opportunity
- **DE (priority 1):** "Made-in-Pforzheim" jewellery heritage + no subscription + GDPR-first data = credible premium niche.
- **US (priority 2):** large demand, but crowded; win with design and gifting, not with health claims.
- **JP (priority 3):** design + small sizes + gift packaging; test via Rakuten first.

## Hand-off
- Core tension: smart rings look like gadgets and cost money every month.
- Price corridor for a design-led, subscription-free ring: **€299–349** [assumption].
- Lead market DE, then US gifting season, JP as test market.
- Avoid medical claims entirely; compete on design, comfort, privacy and battery.
