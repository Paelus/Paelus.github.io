# 02 · Customer Insight – AURUM smart ring

## 1 Personas
| | Primary: "Design-conscious professional" | Secondary: "Gift buyer" |
|---|---|---|
| Who | 28–45, urban, already tried a smartwatch and stopped wearing it at night | Partner/family buying for a birthday or holiday |
| DE | Values Qualität, Datenschutz, timeless design | Wants a premium, explainable gift with no running costs |
| US | Wellness-curious, compares Oura vs. Samsung | Holiday gifting, "no subscription" is an easy sell |
| JP | Prefers understated, small-size jewellery; office-appropriate | Gift packaging and craft story matter [assumption] |

## 2 Jobs-to-be-done & pain points
- "Help me understand my sleep and stress without wearing a bulky watch to bed."
- Pain: rings that look like tech, monthly fees, 3–5-day battery, wrong size after ordering, unclear data use.

## 3 Purchase drivers / barriers
| Driver/Barrier | DE | US | JP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design as jewellery | High | Med | High |
| No subscription | High | High | Med |
| Data privacy (EU servers) | High | Med | Med |
| Battery ≥ 7 days | Med | High | Med |
| Free sizing / easy exchange | High | High | High |
| Price ≤ €349 | Med | Med | Med |

## 4 Feature priority matrix
| Feature | DE | US | JP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sleep score | High | High | High |
| Stress / recovery | High | High | Med |
| Activity / steps | Med | High | Med |
| Gold-tone finish | Med | Med | High |
| Women's small sizes (US 5–7) | Med | Med | High |

## Hand-off
- Message pillar 1: **"Jewellery first, tech inside."**
- Message pillar 2: **"Pay once. No subscription."**
- Message pillar 3: **"Your data stays yours."** (EU hosting)
- Reassurance: free sizing kit + 30-day size exchange.
