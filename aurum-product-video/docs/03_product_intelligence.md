# 03 · Product Intelligence – AURUM smart ring

## 1 Spec sheet  *(all values [concept spec] – not tested)*
| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Material | Grade-5 titanium shell, hypoallergenic resin inner surface |
| Finishes | Brushed silver, satin black, gold-tone (PVD) |
| Sizes | US 5–13, 2.6 mm thin profile, 3.8 g |
| Sensors | PPG heart rate, skin temperature, 3-axis accelerometer |
| Battery | target 7 days, wireless charging case |
| Water | 5 ATM (shower, swimming) |
| App | iOS / Android, sleep score, stress & recovery, activity, no subscription |
| Data | EU hosting, local-first sync, data export, no data sale |
| Price | €329 / $349 / ¥52,800 [assumption] |

## 2 Function → benefit
| Function | Benefit for the customer |
|---|---|
| Sleep tracking | Understand sleep patterns without a watch in bed |
| Stress & recovery trend | See demanding days, plan rest – *wellness insight, not diagnosis* |
| Jewellery finish | Wear it to the office, dinner, or a wedding |
| No subscription | Total cost known on day one |
| EU data hosting | Peace of mind about personal data |

## 3 Usage scenarios
1. Morning: glance at sleep score with coffee.
2. Office: ring looks like jewellery in meetings.
3. Evening run: activity tracked hands-free.
4. Travel to Tokyo: jet-lag sleep trend.
5. Gift moment: unboxing a craft-style case.

## 4 Advantages vs. closest competitors
- vs. **Oura**: no subscription, jewellery finishes.
- vs. **Samsung**: works fully with iPhone and Android.
- vs. **RingConn**: premium design and craft story from Pforzheim.

## 5 Limitations / risks
- Concept product: battery, accuracy and durability unproven.
- Small brand: trust, after-sales service, warranty in JP.
- No medical certification – must not make health claims.
- Thin ring = less battery; 7 days is a target, not a promise.

## Hand-off
- Hero claim set: design, no subscription, privacy, comfort.
- Every spec in the video is shown as "designed for" / "target", never as a measured result.
