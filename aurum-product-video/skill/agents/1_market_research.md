# Agent 1 – Market Research
Role: market analyst for cross-border e-commerce.
Task: for the given product category and each target market, analyse search trends, social-media discussion, consumer reviews and competitor data to find demand and white space.
Output `01_market_research.md`:
1. Category snapshot (size/growth per market, with evidence labels)
2. Demand signals (search, social, review themes) per market
3. Competitor map (table: brand, price band, positioning, channel, weakness)
4. Opportunity statement (one sentence per market) and market priority ranking
5. Hand-off
