# Marketing Copy: Sehwerk

*Prepared by: Copywriter Agent | Based solely on 02-strategy.md | Tone: professional, calm, factual (primary audience: Segment B, professionals and knowledge workers aged about 30–55)*

---

## Slogan

**See more. Film nothing.**

*(German market adaptation for review: "Mehr sehen. Nichts filmen.")*

## Website Copy

### Hero

**Headline:** Smart glasses you can wear anywhere.

**Subheadline:** Sehwerk shows captions, translation and notes in your field of view, with no camera on board, your prescription included and your data processed on the device or on EU servers. Fitted by your optician.

### Key Benefits

**1. Nobody gets filmed.**
Sehwerk has no camera. That is a deliberate choice, not a missing feature. Wear your glasses into meetings, client visits and other people's homes without anyone wondering whether they are being recorded. The people around you stay comfortable, and so do you.

**2. Your glasses, made smarter.**
Most smart glasses treat prescription lenses as an extra. With Sehwerk, single-vision lenses are part of the base product, and varifocals are available. Your optician measures, fits and adjusts them, just like the glasses you already wear.

**3. Your data stays in Europe.**
Processing happens on the device wherever possible, and on EU servers for the rest. Sehwerk is designed GDPR-first from the ground up, so your conversations and notes are not routed through US or Chinese clouds.

**4. Built for how you work, in German.**
Live captions, translation, meeting notes, a discreet teleprompter, notifications and navigation, all in your line of sight. German-language AI is part of the product from day one, not an afterthought.

### CTA

**Primary:** Find a Sehwerk optician near you
**Secondary:** Book a fitting appointment

*Supporting line:* Try them on, have them fitted to your prescription, and see for yourself.

## Pitch Deck Key Points

1. **Problem:** In Germany, privacy is the biggest barrier to smart glasses: 76% of people do not want to be filmed, and 62% would not let a wearer into their home.
2. **Problem:** 64% of Germans aged 16+ (38.7 million people) already wear corrective glasses, yet competitors treat prescription lenses as a paid extra.
3. **Solution:** Sehwerk is camera-free, prescription-first AI display glasses: captions, translation and notes in the field of view, without filming anyone.
4. **Solution:** Sold and fitted through opticians, the channel glasses wearers already trust.
5. **Differentiation:** No camera, prescription and varifocals included, on-device plus EU processing, and German-language AI required at launch. Sehwerk competes on the full package, not on being camera-free alone.
6. **Differentiation:** For retailers, no camera means no recording-related legal risk on the shelf.
7. **Benefit:** Professionals get productivity tools they can use in any meeting, home or clinic, without social friction or data worries.
8. **Benefit:** First-mover window: Sehwerk can establish the "privacy-first, optician-sold" position in Germany before Google/Samsung Android XR glasses reach the EU.
